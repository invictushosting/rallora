# Club sponsors and public pages

Last checked: 21 October 2026.

Open **Club admin → Sponsors** (the shortcut scrolls to **Sponsors & placements**).

1. Add a sponsor name, optional description and website.
2. Choose **Whole club**, **Specific league**, **Specific division** or **Specific event**, then select its target.
3. Upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP logo (up to 5 MB), or paste its image URL.
4. Choose one or more placements: below the header, above tables/event details, beside fixtures/event rounds, or above the footer. These placements apply to the public league hub and events pages.
5. Whole-club sponsors can be designated **Main club sponsor**. Only one active main sponsor is allowed. Remove the old sponsor’s main status before switching.
6. Set display order and leave **Active and visible to players** checked. Save, then open **View public hub** to review. To hide a sponsor, edit it and untick Active.

League, division and event sponsors use prominent logo panels. Switching leagues/events changes the matching sponsors. Main club sponsors remain eligible at their chosen placements; scoped sponsors take precedence in display order. Division-specific placements appear with that division in the hub overview; the division filter also selects sponsors beside fixtures on other views.

Under **Sponsorship enquiries**, set the club’s contact email and choose whether to display invitations. This uses the club’s main contact email. An unsponsored league/event displays a compact invitation above its content; its mail link includes the selected competition name in the subject. Invitations are hidden when disabled, when the contact email is missing/invalid, or when that league/event already has an active matching sponsor. A main club sponsor does not suppress invitations to sponsor an individual league.

Existing sponsors remain available in the partner area above the footer until their placements/scopes are edited. Their existing names, links and artwork are preserved. No sponsor is automatically designated main sponsor.

The club hub header uses a navy foundation with club-colour accents and its existing court image. The shared hub/events footer uses club contact links and the approved Rallora Gold Master artwork.

## Maintenance

When changing sponsor controls or public placement behaviour, update this guide in the same change. Verify saving, reloading, league/event switching, inactive sponsors, email links, and desktop/mobile rendering. No other club’s targets or sponsors may be writable.
