## Article 28 processing terms (draft outline)
1. **Instructions.** Processor handles club competition personal data only on documented lawful instructions, including permitted transfers, unless otherwise required by applicable law; notify controller where permitted.
2. **Confidentiality.** Persons authorised to process personal data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.
3. **Security.** Implement risk-appropriate controls per UK GDPR Article 32: encryption in transit, access restrictions, authentication, backups, logging, recovery and regular testing. Precise measures to be completed from infrastructure audit.
4. **Subprocessors.** Prior general or specific written authorisation, with advance notice of changes and opportunity to object; equivalent contractual obligations and responsibility for subprocessors.
5. **Assistance.** Support controller with rights requests, security, breach notification, impact assessments and supervisory authority consultation, accounting for nature of processing and information available.
6. **Breach cooperation.** Processor notifies controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach, with available details and ongoing updates. Operational notification channel and target deadline [to agree] (avoid conflating with controller's statutory ICO assessment).
7. **Deletion and return.** At controller choice on termination, delete or return personal data, including subprocessors, unless law requires storage; document residual backup timing.
8. **Audit.** Make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance and permit appropriate audits/inspections subject to confidentiality and security.
9. **International transfers.** Identify any transfers outside UK, relevant adequacy regulations, UK IDTA/Addendum or other safeguards as legally required; do not assume UK hosting alone eliminates onward transfers.
10. **Unlawful instruction.** Notify controller immediately if an instruction appears to infringe applicable data protection law.
