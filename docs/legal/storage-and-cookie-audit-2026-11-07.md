## Verification procedure
Using synthetic non-production identities, record first load signed out, login/recovery, registration draft, club admin, Social Studio, logout and shared-device user switch. Record cookie name/domain/path/flags/expiry and storage keys/categories without recording token values or draft personal content. Inspect network initiators and hosting settings for injected trackers. Confirm necessary purposes; for other storage, approve the applicable PECR exception or provide prior consent and an equally usable rejection/withdrawal path where required. Test rejection before optional storage is written.

## Current legal reference
ICO final storage/access guidance (updated 29 April 2026) includes strictly necessary and other purpose-specific exceptions after DUAA. Do not claim every nonessential technology needs consent without assessing the actual exception and its conditions. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/
