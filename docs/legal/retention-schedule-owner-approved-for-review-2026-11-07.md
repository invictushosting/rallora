# Retention schedule — owner-approved proposals for club/legal review

Date: 7 November 2026. Status: OWNER-APPROVED FOR CLUB/LEGAL REVIEW; NOT IMPLEMENTED.

On 7 November 2026, Ste approved the six retention proposals below for incorporation into the compliance drafts and review by clubs/legal counsel. This records business approval of the proposals only: it is not club/controller approval, legal sign-off, permission to delete data or authorisation to publish/deploy.

Approval evidence: in this project conversation, the six-row plain-English schedule was presented with the question “Are you happy to adopt these as the proposed policy for club/legal review?” The owner replied “yes please action”. No approval is inferred for other categories or operating controls.

| Category | Owner-approved proposal | Trigger, purpose and exceptions |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts and core profiles | Review after 12 months without activity; give 90 days' notice before considering deletion | Protect active competition participation, pending requests, obligations and recorded holds. Define reliable activity measurement and notice delivery; separate account removal from sporting history. This is a review/notice process, not deletion at month 12. |
| Ordinary support tickets | Keep for 12 months after closure | Support continuity and follow-up. Unresolved disputes, safeguarding, incidents, claims and legal obligations require separate documented handling. |
| Support screenshots | Review on closure and remove within 90 days when no longer needed | Minimise unnecessary sensitive material sooner; preserve necessary evidence under a recorded hold. This does not guarantee every attachment must remain for 90 days. |
| Successful operational email delivery records | Keep for up to 90 days after successful completion | Delivery troubleshooting. Unresolved failures must be investigated; legally significant notice/acceptance evidence has a separate schedule. This is not approval to delete all notification or message content indiscriminately. |
| Match chat and time proposals | Review 6 months after the competition ends | Allow for sporting disputes; club confirms the appeal/dispute window, lawful purpose and controller instructions. Review retained message text separately from official results. |
| Identifiable names, rosters, results and standings | Initial review 24 months after the season ends | Clubs must justify any longer identifiable sporting archive. Assess removing identifiers or anonymising where appropriate without corrupting official sporting records; pseudonymisation is not automatically anonymisation. |

## Categories still requiring separate decisions
Privacy-rights case evidence, legal acceptance evidence, safeguarding/security incidents, Auth/security/vendor logs, financial records, backups, exports/caches, Social/discovery content and Playtomic-linked records have not received numeric-period approval through this decision. Determine justified category-specific periods, actual provider capabilities and any legal obligations. No blanket default or immediate backup-deletion promise is adopted.

## Club/legal review and implementation
1. Confirm controller/processor allocation and written club instructions, including sporting records, current juniors aged 16–17, dispute windows and exit/return requirements.
2. Review the purpose and justification for each period, applicable rights, holds and any child-specific shorter period/minimisation.
3. Verify actual database relationships, storage objects, mail-provider records and backup/log expiry. Define a non-destructive preview of eligible records, human review, hold protection, notice evidence and audited outcomes before any cleanup implementation.
4. Approve final notices/DPA wording and final retention policy; rehearse any implementation with synthetic records, including active players, team changes, requests and holds.
5. Obtain the concrete implementation/release decision before enabling deletion. This change adds no purge, timer, scheduled task, migration or live user deletion.
