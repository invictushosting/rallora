## 1. Scope
Rallora provides access to a hosted platform to organise padel leagues, fixtures, player registrations, results, standings, and related club communications. Only expressly activated functionality is included. No guarantee of particular features, Playtomic availability, integrations or service levels unless agreed in the order form.

## 2. Roles and club administration
The Club decides sporting regulations, entry criteria, eligibility, match scheduling and appeals. It is responsible for the accuracy of league rules it publishes, appropriate safeguarding and obtaining authority to input information about its players. Rallora operates the software and retains responsibility for its own statutory obligations. Parties must separately map controller and processor roles in the DPA; they cannot be defined by contract contrary to actual practice.

## 3. Users, security and acceptable use
Club shall appoint authorised admins and ensure only properly authorised people use privileged accounts. Parties will promptly report suspected compromised accounts or personal-data incidents. Each party will apply appropriate organisational and technical security.

## 4. Data protection
Data processing schedule / DPA is incorporated only once completed and signed. Neither party may use personal information beyond its documented lawful purposes. Marketing to players and sponsor disclosures are not permitted solely by virtue of this agreement.

## 5. Fees and term
Fees, tax, invoice cadence, renewals, cancellations, suspension, notice period and termination assistance must be completed in an order form; do not apply automatic paid conversion to pilot clubs.

## 6. Availability, support and changes
Support channel: support@rallora.app. Response times, maintenance windows, backup responsibilities, SLA credits and change-notice arrangements **to be negotiated**.

## 7. Intellectual property and publicity
Protego Solutions Ltd retains its software/IP subject to verified ownership and third-party licences. Clubs retain their marks and club-provided materials; any licence to display those materials or identify clubs publicly requires an appropriate agreed permission.

## 8. Responsibility, liability and disputes
Appropriate warranties, indemnities, caps, exclusions, consumer rights (where relevant), applicable law, jurisdiction and escalation processes **to be settled by counsel**. No cap or exclusion has been preselected.

## 9. Exit
Upon termination, provide an agreed, secure export of club-controlled data and delete or return processor-held data subject to lawful retention, backups and DPA terms, within agreed timeframes.
